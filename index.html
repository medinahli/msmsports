About Us

A Different Kind
of Agency

We believe every player deserves a mentor who genuinely knows their game and helps them find the right opportunity — not just the fastest one.

MSM Sports was founded on the belief that football career development should be personal, transparent, and built around the player — not anyone else's interests. Too many talented players fall through the cracks because they don't have the right guidance, the right connections, or because they ended up at the wrong club for the wrong reasons.

Based in the United Kingdom with deep connections across Scandinavian and European football, we specialise in mentoring players toward the perfect environment to develop, compete, and build sustainable careers. Our network spans sporting directors, head coaches, and club executives at every level.

We work closely with a select group of players, giving each one the dedicated attention that larger operations simply cannot offer. From ambitious talent looking to make the leap abroad, to established players seeking new challenges — we help them find the right path.

Our international perspective is a core strength. Understanding the differences between football cultures allows us to position players in environments where they'll genuinely thrive, not just survive.

Services

What We Do

Core Services
What We Offer
I

Opportunity Identification

We help players identify the right clubs and environments for their development. Using our network of sporting directors and coaches, we connect players with genuine opportunities that match their level and ambitions.

II

Trial Coordination

End-to-end management of trial periods: logistics, accommodation guidance, travel, and preparation. We ensure clubs see your best from day one.

III

Contract Guidance

We help players understand what they should be looking for in a deal — salary benchmarks, contract length, key clauses, and how to protect their long-term interests.

IV

Career Strategy

Honest guidance on where you should be playing now versus where you want to be in three years. We map out realistic progression paths.

V

International Pathways

Guidance on moving between football cultures — work permits, registration requirements, and the practical realities of building a career abroad across Europe and Scandinavia.

VI

Player Development

Guidance beyond the contract — connecting players with the right training environments, mentors, and performance support to thrive at their new club.
